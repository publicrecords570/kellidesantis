Document summary

This file contains an Affidavit of Probable Cause concerning Kelli Danielle DeSantis, identified in the document as Kelli Danielle De Santis, and a May 1, 2025 incident in Pike County, Pennsylvania.

According to the affidavit, police received a report of erratic driving on Route 6 and Route 209 from Milford. Officer Langdon subsequently contacted DeSantis in a high school parking lot and reported observing signs consistent with impairment and difficulty performing field sobriety tests.

The affidavit states that DeSantis later attributed her swerving to looking at her laptop. It also states that, after the officer read chemical testing warnings, she remained silent and did not provide consent. The officer documented that response as a refusal to submit to chemical testing.

The document presents police allegations. This two-page file does not include the charge list, a plea, verdict, sentencing order, or final court disposition.

Affidavit of Probable Cause — Transcription

The following transcribes the affidavit’s narrative, continuation, and declarations. Original wording, including apparent typographical errors, is retained; paragraph spacing is adjusted for readability. Filing stamps, handwritten signatures, and blank notarization fields are not reproduced. Refer to the PDF for the source document. Statements below are those of the affiant.

Your Affiant, Officer Langdon, has been a police officer since July 2023 and is certified through the Pennsylvania Municipal Police Officers' Education and Training Commission. Your Affiant has investigated and made arrests related to both felony and misdemeanor offenses.

On Thursday, May 1st, 2025, your Affiant was notified by the Pike County Communication Center (PCCC) regarding a report of a reckless driver traveling in my direction on Rt 6&209from Milford.

PCCC relayed that the complainant, identified as Sherry Vantassel, reported that a black female was operating a black SUV in an erratic manner. Vantassel stated the vehicle nearly struck her ear multiple times. PCCC further advised that the suspect vehicle had entered the high school parking lot and was bearing Pennsylvania registration MWF3189. The vehicle was reported to have parked near the administration building. Upon arrival, I made contact with Vantassel, who pointed out the suspect vehicle.

I approached the vehicle and made contact with the operator, later identified as Kelli Danielle De Santis. I introduced myself and requested her driver's license. De Santis appeared incoherent, was slow to respond, and had difficulty locating her license, which she eventually produced from her wallet. I informed her that I was investigating a report of reckless driving.

While speaking further with De Santis, I observed indicators consistent with impairment, such as seeming lost and incoherent. I asked if she would be willing to perform Standardized Field Sobriety Tests (SFSTs), to which she agreed. De Santis struggled to exit the vehicle and stumbled as she did so. I instructed her to proceed to the front of my patrol vehicle, but she required multiple redirections before complying.

I first administered the Horizontal Gaze Nystagmus (HGN) test. I instructed De Santis to place her feet together, keep her arms at her sides, and follow my pen with her eyes only while keeping her head still. Despite repeated instructions, she was unable to keep her head stationary. She exhibited lack of smooth pursuit, distinct nystagmus at maximum deviation, and onset of nystagmus prior to 45 degrees. Slight vertical nystagmus was also present.

I then administered the Walk and Turn test, explaining and demonstrating the instruction. During which De Santis exhibited numerous indicators, including difficulty following instructions, failing to maintain balance, and improper step execution.

I then administered the One-Leg Stand test, explaining and demonstrating the instructions. De Santis had significant difficulty performing this test. She was unable to maintain her balance and struggled to keep her foot elevated as instructed. Due to her inability to properly perform the test despite multiple attempts, I terminated the test early.

De Santis left a voicemail message on my landline at headquarters. In the message, De Santis stated that the reason she had been swerving on the roadway was because she was looking at her laptop at the time.

I read her the DL-26 Chemical Test Warning, including paragraphs 1 through 4. Upon completion of the warning, De Santis remained silent and did not provide consent. Her lack of response was documented as a refusal to submit to chemical testing.

The above information provided in this affidavit does not include all details known to your Affiant but contains the facts necessary to establish probable cause for the offense(s) described.

I, JUSTIN GLENN LANGDON, BEING DULY SWORN ACCORDING TO THE LAW, DEPOSE AND SAY THAT THE FACTS SET FORTH IN THE FOREGOING AFFIDAVIT ARE TRUE AND CORRECT TO THE BEST OF MY KNOWLEDGE, INFORMATION AND BELIEF.

I CERTIFY THAT THIS FILING COMPLIES WITH THE PROVISIONS OF THE CASE RECORDS PUBLIC ACCESS POLICY OF THE UNIFIED JUDICIAL SYSTEM OF PENNSYLVANIA THAT REQUIRE FILING CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND DOCUMENTS DIFFERENTLY THAN NON-CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND DOCUMENTS.
