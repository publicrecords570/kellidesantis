This file contains an Affidavit of Probable Cause and Police Criminal Complaint concerning Kelli D. DeSantis and a March 23, 2022 crash on Milford Road in Delaware Township, Pike County, Pennsylvania.

Affidavit of Probable Cause — Transcription

The following transcribes the affidavit’s narrative and declarations. Original wording, including apparent typographical errors, is retained; paragraph spacing is adjusted for readability. Handwritten signatures and blank notarization fields are not reproduced. This section does not transcribe the four accompanying complaint pages. Refer to the PDF for the source document. Statements below are those of the affiant.

I, the affiant Tpr. Yuri KISHENKO of the Pennsylvania State Police, Blooming Grove patrol unit, am a duly sworn State Trooper of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

On 03/23/22 at approximately 1655 hours, I was in full uniform in marked patrol unit R4-12. I was dispatched to an MVC on Milford Rd, Delaware Twp, Pike County. Upon arrival, I observed the suspect’s vehicle which was a black in color 2021 Kia Sorento bearing PA registration LJY2195 was crashed into a tree off of the roadway and the suspect was being attended to inside the ambulance by Delaware Township EMS personnel.

On 03/23/22 at approximately 1706 hours, I interviewed DESANTIS at the scene. DESANTIS related she was traveling northbound on Milford Rd when a squirrel ran in front of her car and she swerved right to avoid it, which caused her vehicle to travel off of the roadway and strike a tree with her front bumper. During the interview, I detected the odor of an alcoholic beverage emanating from her person. I observed slurred speech, and bloodshot eyes. Field sobriety tests were not administered due to the smell of an alcoholic beverage and safety concerns due to being in an active crash scene.

DESANTIS was then transported to PSP PSP Blooming Grove barracks in order to provide a breath sample to determine her blood alcohol content. While at PSP Blooming Grove, DESANTIS was advised of her O’CONNELL consent warnings. DESANTIS agreed to submit to a breath test to determine her blood alcohol content (BAC). The test was administered by Tpr. Jessica QUINN using the INTOXILYZER Model 9000. DESANTI’s BAC was determined to be 0.338%.

Your Affiant believes the above mentioned to be true and correct and respectfully requests a summons be issued as per the attached criminal complaint.

I, TPR. YURI KISHENKO BEING DULY SWORN ACCORDING TO THE LAW, DEPOSE AND SAY THAT THE FACTS SET FORTH IN THE FOREGOING AFFIDAVIT ARE TRUE AND CORRECT TO THE BEST OF MY KNOWLEDGE, INFORMATION AND BELIEF.

I CERTIFY THAT THIS FILING COMPLIES WITH THE PROVISIONS OF THE CASE RECORDS PUBLIC ACCESS POLICY OF THE UNIFIED JUDICIAL SYSTEM OF PENNSYLVANIA THAT REQUIRE FILING CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND DOCUMENTS DIFFERENTLY THAN NON-CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND DOCUMENTS.
